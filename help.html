Welcome to Podmix

Podmix is a minimalist music player for Windows, built to be fast, clean, and distraction‑free. It brings a modern twist to the classic “iPod Shuffle” experience—simple controls, instant playback, and a focus on the music itself.

How It Works

Podmix follows a Shuffle‑First philosophy. Instead of managing libraries, sorting folders, or dealing with complicated interfaces, you simply give Podmix your music and it immediately creates a fresh, random sequence for you to enjoy.

Whether you have a small collection or thousands of songs, Podmix is designed to get out of your way and let you listen.

Note: Podmix is optimized for very large music libraries and can shuffle thousands of tracks instantly without slowing down your computer.

Getting Started

Adding Your Music

There are two easy ways to start listening:

  • Drag and Drop: Grab any folder of music from your computer and drop it onto the player. Podmix will instantly load it and begin playing.
  • Right-Click Menu: Right-click anywhere on the player and choose “Add Files” or “Add Folder” to browse manually.

Song Ratings

Podmix includes a simple but powerful rating system that helps the player understand your taste. The more you rate your music, the smarter your shuffle becomes.

How to Rate a Song

While a song is playing, double-click the artwork to open the rating overlay. You’ll see five stars:

  • Click a star to set the rating (1–5 stars).
  • Hover over stars to preview your rating before selecting.
  • Double-click the artwork again to close the rating view.

How Ratings Affect Playback

Ratings directly influence how Podmix chooses what to play next. Higher-rated songs become more likely to appear earlier in a shuffle, while lower-rated songs appear less often. This creates a personalized listening experience that adapts to you.

Smart Shuffle Explained

Every time you reshuffle, Podmix uses a weighted system to decide the order of songs:

  • 5 Stars: Very likely to play early and often
  • 4 Stars: More likely to play
  • 3 Stars: Neutral
  • 2 Stars: Less likely to play
  • 1 Star: Rarely played
  • Unrated: Treated as neutral until you rate them

This ensures that:

  • Your favorite songs rise to the top of each shuffle
  • Every song still has a chance to appear
  • No song repeats until the entire playlist has been played

Why Rating Matters

The more songs you rate, the better Podmix understands your taste. Over time, your shuffle becomes uniquely tuned to you—perfect for rediscovering favorites or avoiding songs you don’t enjoy.

Instant Re-Shuffle with Ratings

After rating a few songs, you can instantly generate a new personalized order by double-clicking the control wheel. Podmix will immediately create a fresh shuffle that prioritizes your highest-rated tracks.

Managing Playlists

Podmix remembers what you were listening to. You can save your current shuffle sequence as a playlist to revisit later, or create manual playlists by adding specific songs to a collection.

Instant Artist Mix

If a song comes on and you want to hear more from that artist, open the screen view and right-click the artist’s name. Podmix will instantly create a playlist of only that artist’s music.

Instant Re-Shuffle

If you want to mix things up, simply double-click anywhere on the control wheel. The player will immediately generate a brand new random order for your music.

Music Downloader

Podmix includes a powerful downloader that allows you to save your favorite tracks and playlists directly from YouTube and YouTube Music for offline listening.

How to Use

Press the D key to open the Music Downloader window. You can toggle between two modes:

  • Songs Mode: Type the name of a song or artist to see the top search results. Select the ones you want and add them to your queue.
  • Playlist Mode: Paste a direct link to a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist. Podmix will fetch all tracks automatically.
    • Note: When downloading music, it is recommended to use the Playlist Mode and YouTube Music playlist links because you will have more control over the results. Using the Songs Mode might show unwanted results (e.g. movies, concerts, and the like). If you have a playlist link, either from YouTube or YouTube Music, you will know for a fact that you will be downloading songs.

Playlist Organization

When you download a playlist, Podmix automatically creates a new folder named after the playlist inside your Podmix Downloads directory which is located in C:\Users\YourUsername\Music\Podmix Downloads. This keeps your library organized and prevents tracks from different albums or collections from getting mixed up.

Automatic MP3 Conversion

The downloader fetches the highest quality audio available. Once a track finishes downloading, the app uses FFmpeg to instantly convert the file to high-quality MP3 format. This ensures maximum compatibility with all your devices. The temporary source files are automatically cleaned up to save space. In case Podmix cannot clean traces of temporary M4A format files, you can delete them manually.

Why a download might fail:

While the downloader is highly robust, you may occasionally encounter an error. This usually happens for the following reasons:

  • Privacy Settings: The playlist must be set to Public or Unlisted. Private playlists cannot be accessed by the app.
  • Regional Restrictions: Some official artist tracks are licensed only for specific countries. If the content is blocked in your region, the download will fail.
  • Bot Challenges: YouTube occasionally triggers security challenges (like CAPTCHAs) on official music albums to prevent automated downloads.
  • Age Restrictions: Content marked as "18+" or sensitive may require a verified account login, which the downloader does not support for privacy reasons.

External Music Sources

While the built-in downloader is highly convenient, you may occasionally want to download high-quality albums or full discographies from other websites. These options are often superior when it comes to MP3 Tags (Metadata), as the files typically include embedded artist, album, and artwork information that Podmix can read automatically.

Recommended external sources:

  • SpotMate: Download Spotify songs in MP3 format. Playlist downloads require Premium access which you can get by donating an amount to the developers to keep the service online (may contain Ads).
  • Discografias Completas: A comprehensive Spanish website for full discographies in different languages (contains Ads).
  • Discografias Completas Full: Another extensive Spanish website for full music collections in different languages, too (contains Ads).
  • HOME BRONCO: One more time, a comprehensive Spanish website for downloading discographies in several languages (contains Ads).
  • Internet Archive (Audio): A massive library of free, legal audio including live concerts and public domain music.
  • Metal Music Blog (Darkport): A dedicated source for metal music fans. (Note: Contains advertisements).

Additional Information: Please note that these websites typically provide a password on their page to extract the compressed archives you download. Some links utilize URL shorteners that may require a VPN connection to bypass regional blocks or access the destination. If you experience slow speeds once you have passed the advertisements and reached the final download link, you can disconnect your VPN and restart the download to utilize your full connection speed.

Tip: Using these external sources is the best way to ensure your library looks perfect. Because these files come with pre-configured tags, Podmix will be able to display your music nicely and show the correct high-resolution covers without needing to search the web for them.

Internet Radio

Podmix allows you to stream thousands of radio stations from around the world.

The Internet Radio service is powered by radio-browser.info. Special thanks to the creator, Alex Segler, and the community for maintaining this open database. If you can, please consider supporting his work.

Radio Features

  • Live Updates: Track titles and artists update automatically as the station plays.
  • Smart Artwork: The player automatically finds the correct album covers for songs playing on the radio.
  • Auto-Reconnect: If your internet blinks, the player will try to restart the station for you automatically.
  • Block HTTP Streams: For your security, you can choose to only show radio stations that use secure, encrypted connections. This filters out older, insecure web addresses from your search results to ensure a safer streaming experience.

Recording

You can record any live radio broadcast to listen to later. Right-click the player while a station is playing and choose "Recording." You can save the audio as a high-quality music file directly to your computer.

Note: While you listen to the radio, Podmix automatically searches the internet to try to find the correct song names and album covers. This ensures your radio experience looks just as good as your local music library, even on stations that don't provide their own information. Please note that metadata accuracy depends on the information provided by the radio station and may occasionally vary.

Lyrics & Search

Finding Songs

Press the S key to open the Search window. You can search through thousands of songs instantly by Title, Artist, or Album automatically.

Viewing Lyrics

Press the L key to see the lyrics for the current song. If the lyrics are found online, you can choose to save them permanently to your music file so they are always available, even when you are offline.

Customizing the Player

Dynamic Colors

Podmix can automatically match its appearance to the artwork of the song you're playing. Enable "Extract From Album Cover" in the settings, and the entire player—along with your desktop notifications—will adapt to the dominant colors of the current track.

If you prefer a consistent look, you can also choose from a set of predefined color themes available in the menu.

System Tray & Notifications

Click the Green Button to hide Podmix from your taskbar. The player will continue running quietly in the system tray near your clock.

Whenever a new song starts while the app is minimized or hidden, Podmix will display a small notification so you always know what’s playing without reopening the player.

Discord Rich Presence

Podmix can display your currently playing music as a Rich Presence activity on your Discord profile. This lets your friends see what you're listening to while you enjoy your music.

To enable this feature, right-click anywhere on the player and select "Discord Listening Status" from the context menu. Select it again to disable the integration.

Note: The Discord desktop application must be installed and running for Rich Presence to work. If Discord is unavailable, the feature will not be displayed in the player menu.
